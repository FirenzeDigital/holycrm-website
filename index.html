Questions pastors ask

Good to know

Will my team actually use it?

That's the whole point of the design. Each person sees a short menu of just what they need, it looks the same on a phone as on a computer, and there's nothing to install. Most volunteers are comfortable after one Sunday.

We're not very technical. Is that a problem?

No. We set your church up for you and help you bring in your lists. Day to day, it's filling in simple forms — closer to a tidy address book than to accounting software.

Can leaders use it on their phones?

Yes. It works in any phone browser, and you can add it to the home screen so it opens like a normal app — handy for a group leader taking attendance or a greeter checking who's serving.

Is it built for our kind of church?

It's made for evangelical and protestant churches — one congregation or a whole network. The words it uses ('culto', 'pastor', 'líder', 'célula', 'ofrenda') fit that world.

We already have spreadsheets. Can we bring them in?

Usually yes. Send us your member and group lists and we'll help you import them so you don't start from scratch.

Is our congregation's information safe?

Your church's data is walled off from every other church and protected by roles you control. We don't sell or share it, and you can export or delete it whenever you ask.
