From first visit to family

Follow Hannah through her first months at Grace Community Church. Every screen below is HolyCRM.app.

  1. 1Sunday · first visit

    She scans the QR code during the service

    Your church's page opens on her phone: the live service, how to give, a prayer request — and a “New here?” card she fills in before she leaves.

    • One link for Instagram, the bulletin and the screen
    • Her card reaches your welcome team right away
  2. 2Monday · follow-up

    Someone calls her — and it's written down

    Hannah is assigned to someone on the welcome team. The thank-you WhatsApp, the call, the visit to her home: every conversation stays on her record, so nobody has to remember.

    • Every guest has a person responsible, not a group chat
    • The funnel shows who's still waiting for a call
  3. 3Week 3 · a small group

    She finds her people on Thursday nights

    She joins the Downtown Group. Its leader logs each meeting — who came, who visited, the offering — in a minute, from the phone.

    • See which groups are growing and ready to multiply
    • Leaders, supervisors and hosts, all in one place
  4. 4Month 2 · serving

    She serves — and confirms with one tap

    Hannah is a greeter this Sunday. She gets a link by email or WhatsApp and answers “I'll be there” — no app, no password. Your coordinator sees every answer, and every open spot, for the week.

    • Week by week, with the open spots highlighted
    • A warning before anyone is booked twice
  5. 5Month 2 · giving

    Her giving is recorded, with her name

    Tithes and offerings are kept per person and per category, in your currency. At the end of the year, each giver's statement is one click away.

    • A finance dashboard for your treasurer
    • Each member can see their own giving history
  6. 6Every Monday · the big picture

    The pastor sees the whole church at a glance

    New members this month, guests waiting for a call, teams still short this Sunday, birthdays coming up. Hannah is one of the numbers going up.

    • Everyone sees only what their role allows
    • Works on any phone, in English, Spanish and Portuguese

Questions pastors ask

Good to know

Will my team actually use it?

That's the whole point of the design. Each person sees a short menu of just what they need, it looks the same on a phone as on a computer, and there's nothing to install. Most volunteers are comfortable after one Sunday.

We're not very technical. Is that a problem?

No. We set your church up for you and help you bring in your lists. Day to day, it's filling in simple forms — closer to a tidy address book than to accounting software.

Can leaders use it on their phones?

Yes. It works in any phone browser, and you can add it to the home screen so it opens like a normal app — handy for a group leader taking attendance or a greeter checking who's serving.

Is it built for our kind of church?

Most likely, yes. It works for a church plant of 30 and for a congregation of thousands, for one location or a network with several campuses. You turn on only what you use — cells, ministries, serving teams, giving — and leave the rest off. Every church calls its roles differently ('líder', 'guía', 'maestro'…), so you use your own words and the app follows. If you're not sure, write to us and we'll tell you honestly whether it fits.

We already have spreadsheets. Can we bring them in?

Usually yes. Send us your member and group lists and we'll help you import them so you don't start from scratch.

Is our congregation's information safe?

Your church's data is walled off from every other church and protected by roles you control. We don't sell or share it, and you can export or delete it whenever you ask.

What happens after my 30-day trial?

Nothing is deleted. If you don't upgrade, your church automatically continues on the Free plan — up to 50 people, 2 team members and 1 location, with members, visitors, groups, attendance and events. Anything above those limits stays visible but read-only until you either trim it back or upgrade to HolyCRM.app.
